4. Checkpoints, schedules and payroll limits

Compatible NFC cards and QR badges are identifiers, not proof of physical identity. Cards and static badges may be shared or copied. The business must choose appropriate supervision and review processes; Staffly does not provide facial recognition or continuous employee location tracking.

Administrators must review missed scans, open shifts, breaks, corrections and approved payable time. Attendance insights are advisory comparisons with configured schedules; they are not legal absence determinations or automatic authorization for deductions or overtime. Leave allowances and working rules are business-configured and do not establish statutory entitlements.

Payroll calculates gross wages and records payments already made. Staffly does not transfer salaries, calculate statutory taxes or contributions, file declarations or provide legal, tax or accounting advice. Businesses must verify results and arrange required deductions, filings and payments separately. Currency and timezone settings do not establish compliance with a country's payroll rules.

5. Local records, security and backups

The workplace device is the authority for local business records. Use an appropriate device lock, supported authentication or a Staffly PIN, and safeguard recovery information. Maintain current encrypted backups and keep their passwords separately. We cannot recover forgotten backup passwords or missing device-held keys, remotely unlock a workplace database or guarantee recovery after loss, uninstalling, clearing app storage or device damage.

Generated documents saved or shared outside Staffly become readable copies controlled by the destination or recipient. Application protection does not protect those external copies. Backups, reports, employee enrollments and subscriptions have different restoration requirements; consult the app's explanations before restoring or replacing a device. Security controls reduce risk but do not guarantee protection on a compromised or unattended unlocked device.

6. Optional employee access

Where released and enabled, an administrator can enroll an employee's personal device. Compare and confirm the connection codes on both devices. Employees may access only their permitted personal records and badge. Nearby refresh requires compatible devices, requested permissions, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi; foreground interaction may be needed. No router is required for the nearby transfer, but it is not a remote-home or guaranteed background service.

The personal device shows the latest successfully downloaded snapshot, not a live connection to the employer's records. Changes remain unavailable until a new synchronization succeeds. Review publication/download timestamps before relying on the view. Employee consultation does not submit leave requests or edit authoritative workplace records.

Revocation prevents future downloads but cannot remotely erase previously downloaded snapshots, saved badges or exported reports from an offline device. Businesses must explain permitted use and retention. Employees must protect their personal access and follow lawful workplace rules. Deleting or disconnecting a personal copy does not delete the employer's records.
