## 1. About this policy

Staffly is a workplace application for managing employee profiles, attendance, schedules, leave and gross payroll on a shared business phone or tablet. This policy explains how the
Android application handles personal information, including local workplace
records, optional advertising, subscriptions, and support communications.

In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Laissaoui Mohammed Lotfi, the publisher
of Staffly. “Business administrator” means the person or organization managing the
workplace device and its employee records.

**Core HR records are stored locally. Advertising and subscription services use
internet connections and process separate service-related information.** Staffly
does not provide HR cloud synchronization or require a remote HR account.

### Optional nearby employee access

Where enabled by the business, Staffly can enroll an employee's personal phone
through an administrator-issued invitation and a connection code compared on both
devices. The business device sends only that employee's permitted attendance,
breaks, schedule, leave/balances, identity labels, optional small profile-photo
thumbnail and QR badge to an encrypted cache on the enrolled phone. Payroll
amounts, internal notes, contact details and other employees' records are excluded.
The employee can read the last downloaded snapshot offline and save readable badge
or attendance exports. This is nearby device-to-device transfer, not HR cloud
storage or remote synchronization at home.

Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and camera permissions support discovery, direct transfer and QR
scanning. On older Android versions and on iOS hotspot connections, the operating
system may also require location-related permission; Staffly does not request
location updates or implement employee location tracking. Android Nearby uses
Google Play services, whose connection performance diagnostics and device
information may be processed under Google's usage and diagnostics settings; see
[Google Nearby data collection](https://developers.google.com/nearby/connections/overview#data_collection).
Employee mode does not initialize Staffly's advertising or subscription services.

The administrator controls enrollment and revocation. Revocation prevents future
downloads but cannot erase snapshots or exported files already on an offline
phone. Employees can disconnect to remove their local employee cache; separately
exported copies must be deleted separately. Restoring a workplace backup requires
fresh employee enrollment and badge issuance. A static QR badge can be copied; it
is a checkpoint identifier and does not authorize downloading personal records.
Businesses must explain personal-device access, permitted use and retention to
employees before enabling this optional feature.

## 2. Responsibility for workplace information

The business using Staffly determines why employee information is entered, who may
access it, and how long workplace records should be kept. Where applicable data
protection law uses this terminology, that business is the controller of those
workplace records. It must provide appropriate employee notices, establish a lawful
basis, and follow employment and record-retention requirements that apply to it.

We provide the application and are responsible for the publisher-side processing
described here, including subscription administration and communications sent to
us. We do not receive or remotely administer your locally stored employee database
through Staffly's normal operation.

Employees seeking access, corrections, or deletion of their workplace records
should contact their employer or business administrator first. We cannot retrieve
or remotely delete records that remain solely on their workplace device.

## 3. Information handled by Staffly

### Workplace records stored on the device

Depending on what an administrator enters, Staffly handles:

- Business name, language, currency, timezone, and checkpoint preferences.
- Employee names, employee numbers, contact details, departments, job titles,
  employment dates, notes, profile photographs, and active or archived status.
- NFC and QR credential identifiers, assignments, replacements, revocations, and history.
- Gross payroll rates, calculated earnings, adjustments, finalized wage records and recorded payments.
- Clock-in and clock-out records, timestamps, checkpoint methods, recorded timezone,
  corrections, reasons, and related audit history.

Leave records may include requested dates, paid/unpaid categories, full/half-day
units, approval/rejection/cancellation reasons, manual allocations and adjustment
history. These workplace records stay in the encrypted local HR database and
administrator-created backups; leave reports may be exported on request. Avoid
entering medical diagnoses or unnecessary sensitive details in free-text reasons.

Work schedule records may include weekly hours, employee assignments, dated
exceptions, revision history and administrative reasons. Attendance insights are
calculated locally from these records. They remain in the encrypted local HR
database and administrator-created backups; schedule reports may be exported.

These records support employee organization, credential recognition, attendance
review, corrections, and administrator-requested backup and restoration. Optional
profile fields should contain only information needed for workplace administration.

### Authentication and recovery information

When you choose device authentication, Android checks the supported biometric or
device credential. Staffly receives an authentication result; it does not receive
fingerprint templates, your device PIN, or your device unlock pattern.

If you configure a Staffly PIN, the app stores salted verification values and
retry-control information in platform-protected storage. Recovery-code verification
information and device-held encryption keys are also stored locally. Do not send
your PIN, recovery code, encryption keys, or backup passwords to support.

### Information from network services

Advertising and subscription providers handle the technical and transaction
information explained in sections 5 and 6. This information is separate from
employee identities and attendance records.

### Support communications

If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments
you choose to send. We use them to respond, troubleshoot, and handle privacy
requests. Avoid sending identifiable employee records, photos, card identifiers,
or unredacted backups. If a screenshot is needed, remove unnecessary personal data.

## 4. Photos, NFC, and the shared checkpoint

Staffly launches the device camera when an administrator requests a profile photo.
Accepted photos are processed locally; the saved profile copy is resized and
stripped of source metadata, including embedded location information. The operating
system or camera application may create temporary files before processing. Staffly
attempts to remove its temporary captures after handling them, but interruption or
operating-system behavior can affect cleanup. Copies created separately by a camera
application are subject to that application's settings.

Staffly reads a supported NFC card's identifier for employee credential enrollment
and attendance recognition. It does not use this workflow to read the contents of
a national identity document or extract its printed government identification
number. A card identifier is not proof of who is holding the card. Staffly does not
perform employee facial recognition or generate facial matching templates.

The shared checkpoint briefly displays a recognized employee's name, checkpoint
result, and profile photo where available. People near the device may see this
confirmation. The app clears the feedback after approximately five seconds and
when backgrounded. Businesses should position the device appropriately and explain
this checkpoint display to employees.

## 5. Advertising and privacy choices

The free application may display Google AdMob banners and native ads on selected
administrator screens, interstitial ads at designated administrator workflow
breaks, and App Open ads during eligible startup or foreground-return transitions
when those formats are enabled. Checkpoint scans, authentication, and backup
operations do not trigger ads. Staffly Pro removes all Staffly ad placements.

Google's advertising services may receive IP addresses, approximate location inferred
from IP, advertising or app/device identifiers, app/ad interaction events, and
performance information. These services use this information for ad delivery,
measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. Available identifiers and ad
personalization depend on device settings, regional requirements, and your choices.

**We do not supply employee names, contact details, photos, NFC identifiers, or
attendance records for advertising or ad targeting. We do not sell local HR records.**

Staffly uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain advertising privacy choices
where required and requests ads only when that platform permits them. Where required,
a privacy-options entry is available in the Staffly Pro administrator area. You
can also use Android's advertising-ID and privacy controls. Withdrawing consent
applies to future processing that depends on that consent; it does not invalidate
processing already lawfully completed. Refusing advertising consent does not disable
core staff management, attendance, or backups.

Learn more: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Google advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 6. Subscriptions and purchases

Optional Staffly Pro subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing and
managed with RevenueCat. Purchase tokens, product and subscription information,
transaction history, entitlement status, app-user identifiers, and relevant app or
device information are processed to validate access, handle renewals, prevent
purchase misuse, and restore eligible subscriptions.

Staffly uses a RevenueCat-generated subscription identifier rather than employee
identities. We do not configure employee contact attributes or HR records in
RevenueCat. Its subscription records may be accessible to us through the publisher's
RevenueCat dashboard for subscription administration and support.

Google Play handles payment credentials. Staffly does not receive your full payment
card number or card security code. Restoring a subscription requires the appropriate
store account and internet access; it does not restore employee records.

Subscription status may be cached locally for bounded offline use. This cache is
separate from HR backups. Uninstalling Staffly or clearing its data does not cancel
a subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play.

Learn more: [RevenueCat privacy information](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and
[Google Play subscription management](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).

## 9. Storage, security, and backups

Staffly encrypts persisted HR records and saved profile attachments in its private
application storage. Device-held keys and administrator verification information
use platform-protected storage. Administrator screens lock on backgrounding,
explicit locking, and inactivity. Processing in device memory and camera temporary
files may occur before encrypted persistence.

These controls reduce risk but cannot guarantee protection against a compromised
operating system, unauthorized device access, shared administrator credentials, or
someone observing an unlocked screen. Use a strong device lock and restrict who
knows or can use administrator authentication.

Administrator-requested HR backups are encrypted and protected by a user-held backup
password. Exported copies are outside the app's private storage and may be retained
by a chosen device, storage service, or recipient. Protect those copies and their
passwords separately. We cannot recover lost device keys or backup passwords.

Generated Excel, CSV, and PDF reports are stored in an encrypted, device-local
Documents library. They are excluded from HR backups and cleared when a backup is
restored. Opening a report in another app or saving a copy provides a readable,
unencrypted document to that app or chosen destination. Temporary viewing copies
are removed on return to Staffly or at its next startup. External copies remain
under the chosen app, storage provider, or recipient's control. Deleting a document
inside Staffly removes only its library copy.

Imported HR backups do not transfer administrator authentication or establish
subscription authority. A device change requires both appropriate local data
restoration and, where applicable, separate subscription restoration.

Staffly keeps the current and previous encrypted database snapshots for local recovery. A previous snapshot can retain recently changed or removed data, including a replaced profile photo, until another successful save retires it. Interrupted or damaged snapshots may be retained to avoid destructive recovery. Recovering a previous snapshot requires administrator authentication and confirmation; recent changes may be missing.

## 10. Retention and deletion

**Local workplace records:** Records remain under the business administrator's
control. Archiving an employee retains their profile and historical attendance;
archiving is not deletion. Corrections and credential revocations may retain audit
history. Administrators must establish an appropriate retention schedule.

**Deleting the local installation's data:** An authorized device owner can clear
Staffly's app storage in Android Settings or uninstall Staffly to remove its local
application data and device-held keys. Export any records you lawfully need to
retain first. Separately exported backups, camera-app copies, recipient copies,
and provider-held information must be managed separately. Restoring an older backup
can reintroduce records it contains.

**Subscription and service records:** Provider records may be retained for service
operation, transaction history, fraud prevention, or legal obligations. Removing
local HR data does not automatically erase Google Play or RevenueCat records.
Contact us about publisher-managed subscription information; Google account and
payment-related requests may also need to be directed to Google.

**Support records:** We retain support communications while needed to address the
request and relevant follow-up, disputes, security matters, or legal obligations.
They are not kept for an indefinite unrelated purpose. Contact us to request deletion
of support information, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Staffly does not create an online HR account whose deletion would remove records
from an employer's device.
