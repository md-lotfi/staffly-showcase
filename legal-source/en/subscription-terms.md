## 2. What Staffly Pro includes

**Staffly Pro removes advertisements from Staffly while your subscription is
active and verified.** It is an ongoing ad-free subscription, not a lifetime
purchase.

Core HR features implemented in your installed version remain available without a
subscription. These include staff management, supported checkpoint methods,
attendance review and breaks, schedules, leave, gross payroll, supported reports,
administrator security, local profile photos and encrypted backups. Optional
employee access depends on release availability and workplace configuration. Pro
removes advertisements; it does not add a separate HR feature tier, facial
recognition, HR cloud synchronization or a cloud backup service. Purchasing Pro
does not enable a feature that has not been implemented in your app version.

## 5. Managing and cancelling your subscription

To manage or cancel on Android:

1. Open Google Play using the account that purchased Staffly Pro.
2. Tap your profile icon, then **Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.
3. Select **Staffly Pro** and follow the management or cancellation instructions.

You can also use [Google Play's subscription management page](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).
Staffly's subscription screen provides a management link where available.

Cancellation normally stops future renewal while preserving Pro access until the
end of the paid period. Refunds, revoked purchases, or other store-reported status
changes may end access earlier. When Pro ends, advertisements may return; your
local staff and attendance records remain available.

**Uninstalling Staffly, clearing app storage, deleting workplace records, or
stopping use of the app does not cancel your subscription.** See
[Google Play's cancellation guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481?hl=en).
